# Game Rules

## The aim

Every base has a flag. Find an enemy's flag, pick it up with a jeep and bring it home. The last team standing wins.

## How a base goes out

A base is out of the match when either of these happens:

- **Its flag is captured.** An enemy jeep carries the flag home. Home is the jeep's own base, or the base of any teammate still in the match.
- **It has no jeeps left.** None in the bunker and none on the map. A base that cannot capture a flag cannot win, so it is out.

A player whose base is out stays in the match as a watcher. Their view follows a teammate who is still playing.

## How a match ends

- The last team with a base in the match wins.
- If the last bases go out at the same moment, the match is a draw.
- A match stops after two hours. The team with the most jeeps left then wins, and equal counts make it a draw.

## The flag

- Each base's flag starts hidden in one of its flag buildings, picked at random. Destroying that building reveals it.
- Only a jeep can carry a flag. Drive over it to pick it up. The third button puts it down or picks it up again.
- If the jeep is destroyed, the flag drops where it was.
- A jeep that brings its **own** side's flag home hides it again, in a flag building that is still standing if there is one.

## Vehicles

You drive one vehicle at a time, launched from the lift in your bunker. Each map sets how many of each you get. A common set is 8 jeeps, 3 tanks, 3 ASVs and 3 helicopters, and some maps give unlimited numbers.

| Vehicle | Good at | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jeep** | The only vehicle that carries a flag. Fast, throws grenades at vehicles close by, and can blow up its tyres to float across deep water | Lightly armoured. Lose them all and your base is out |
| **Tank** | Shelling buildings and gun towers. Its turret turns separately from the hull, it drives over rocks and crushes bushes and tents, and it can raise its fire at aircraft | Slow |
| **ASV** | Armoured support. Fires at ground targets and raised at aircraft, and lays mines that arm, blink and blast | Carries a limited number of mines |
| **Helicopter** | Crossing the map quickly and scouting. Carries a gun and missiles, strafes sideways, and only anti-air fire reaches it in the air | Has to wind up before it lifts off, and must land on its lift to dock |

### Fuel, ammunition and damage

- Every vehicle burns fuel and carries limited ammunition. A vehicle out of fuel stops where it is and its driver runs.
- Drive onto any depot's pad to refuel and rearm.
- Drive back onto your own base and press the bunker key to dock. A vehicle you bring home goes back into your count.
- A vehicle that is destroyed is gone for the match.

## The battlefield

- **Buildings** take damage and fall. When nearly destroyed they let their soldiers out. Prisons let out the other side's men.
- **Gun towers** wake when an enemy comes near, track one target and hold fire while their own side is in the way.
- **Gates** slide open for their own side.
- **Soldiers** run from vehicles, swim and throw grenades. They can be run over or shot.
- **Mines** lie at random on some maps, besides the ones ASVs lay.
- **Attack drones** come for a vehicle that sits still for six seconds, from difficulty 4 up.
- **Water:** vehicles wade through shallows and sink in deep water. The jeep can float across on its tyres.
- **Shots fly in 3D.** Tank shells pass over rocks and men, and ground fire only reaches a helicopter when it is raised at it.
- **The edge of the map:** drive far outside it and you will not be left alone.

## Teams and bases

Maps have two, three or four bases, coloured Brown, Green, Blue and Red. On three and four base maps you pick a team layout: 2v2, 2v1, 2v1v1, 3v1 and so on, or everyone for themselves.

The computer plays every base that has no player. A base left out of a match altogether (a closed seat in a lobby) is on nobody's side: its towers stay silent and its men treat every vehicle as an enemy.

## Handicap

The handicap is an option for uneven teams, off by default. It helps a team with fewer bases than the biggest in two ways:

- **More vehicles.** Its counts are scaled up in proportion and rounded up: double for one base against two, triple for one against three.
- **More captures needed.** Its flag must be captured more than once: three times for one base against two, five times for one against three. After a capture that is not the last, the flag goes back to its base and is hidden again.

Even with the handicap the smaller side is still the underdog.

## Computer players

- Skill runs from 1 to 9, and 5 is the default.
- Bots shell flag buildings, clear the gun towers round a flag before sending a jeep, float jeeps across water, hunt a jeep that is carrying their flag, and go home to repair.
- Bots wait in their bunkers until every human player has launched a vehicle, or 45 seconds after the first did, so nobody is overrun while still choosing.
- With three or four sides, each team of bots picks its target afresh every minute, so no base is always attacked first.

## Fastest wins

Training on a built-in map records your time when you win with no handicap. Signed in, the time goes on that map's leaderboard once the server has replayed your run and seen you win.
