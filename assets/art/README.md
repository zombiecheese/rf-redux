# The project's own art

- `cels/N.png`: a sprite drawn by hand, replacing sprite (cel) number N.
- `pack/`: the art pack the game runs on, built by `npm run art`. Commit it together with the change that produced it.

Every picture and sound here is made for Return Fire: Redux. Nothing from the original game, or converted from it,
may be put here, beyond two things: its measurements (`pack/*.json`) and its 104
two-player map layouts (`pack/maps/`).

The modern view's ground, rocks and the sky its toys reflect are the one exception: photo scans and sky photos published as CC0, credited in [pack/cc0](pack/cc0/README.md).

[docs/assets.md](../../docs/assets.md) describes the pack, how each part is made and those two things.
